## Order to generate

1. **Character pictures** for the 7 characters in `01-characters.md`, then one Element each in Kling, named exactly like the tags: `@FatherA`, `@MotherA`, `@BoyA`, `@FatherB`, `@MotherB`, `@GirlB`, `@Guard`.
2. **A1 and A3** (2 credits). Upload them to `demo/raw/case-A/`. **Stop here**: I test them in CreativAI first.
3. If the early test passes: **A2, A4, A5, A6** (4 credits).
4. **B1–B6** (6 credits). Upload them to `demo/raw/case-B/` and don't look at results on them (blind test, see `demo/test-plan.md`).
5. **Spares** (3 credits): redo any clip that failed the checklist below; if none failed, make A7, then A8.

## Check every clip before accepting it (a failed clip costs one spare)
- The camera **doesn't move** (no pan, zoom or shake).
- The characters' **clothes stay the same colours** for the whole clip, and match their pictures.
- The **key moment happens** (for example, in A3 the boy really leaves the store, and in A6 the boy never leaves the table).
- No text, logos or watermark burned into the picture.
- Nothing grotesque (melting faces, extra limbs). Small imperfections are fine; it's security footage.

## Settings
Kling 3.0 · 16:9 · 15 seconds · Elements on (use the "With Elements" prompt) · no audio needed.
If you can't use Elements, use the "Without Elements" prompt and give the character's front photo as the reference or start image.
